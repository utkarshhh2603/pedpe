This project focuses on designing a Digital Monitoring, Reporting, and Verification (dMRV) system to support climate mitigation efforts through reliable carbon monitoring and carbon credit generation. The system aims to enable individuals, farmers, and organizations to participate in carbon sequestration activities such as plantation and land restoration, while ensuring transparent verification and reporting.

The project is being developed as part of a research study on digital MRV systems and carbon markets.

Traditional MRV systems rely heavily on manual data collection and periodic verification, which often leads to:

High operational costs

Limited transparency

Data inconsistencies

Low participation from small landowners and farmers

There is a need for a technology-driven MRV platform that enables real-time monitoring, automated reporting, and scalable verification.
Project Objectives

Design a digital MRV framework for carbon monitoring

Enable continuous monitoring of plantation and land-based carbon sequestration

Provide transparent reporting and verification mechanisms

Support carbon credit generation and utilization

Encourage participation from farmers, landowners, and industries
The primary outcome of this project is a research paper describing the design and implementation of a digital MRV framework for carbon monitoring and carbon credit systems.
A prototype platform demonstrating the core workflow of monitoring, reporting, and verification may also be developed.
