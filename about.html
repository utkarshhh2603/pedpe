About PedPay

Empowering sustainable action through verified carbon credits

Our Mission

PedPay is committed to combating climate change by creating a transparent, accessible marketplace for carbon credits. We connect plantation owners, environmental organizations, and businesses to accelerate the transition to a carbon-neutral future.

For NGOs

Register your reforestation and conservation projects. Get verified carbon credits for your environmental work and access funding from organizations committed to sustainability.

For Verifiers

Join our network of accredited verifiers. Use satellite imagery and field verification to validate plantations and issue certified carbon credits.

For Companies

Offset your carbon footprint by purchasing verified credits. Demonstrate your commitment to sustainability with transparent, traceable carbon credits.
